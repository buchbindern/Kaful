# M8. Scope

## The problem it addressed
Legacy triage decided full models after per-component LLM work had already been paid for, and on tiny_pump it
kept 11 full models for a skid the manual says has two main components. M8 adds the scope phase (p3): it
decides, before any per-component work, which components get a full degradation model, which stay as
connections, and which are excluded. It does so by deterministic rules over what understand already produced,
with no LLM call, and every decision is reviewable and overridable.

## What was built
- **Scope schema 2** (`kaful/artifacts/scope.py`). Each decision stores its reason, `decided_by` (rule or
  override), review flags and the signals it rests on:
  - **G** `fault_modes`: documented gradual fault modes (verified quote; not downgraded, not llm_judgement).
  - **O**, three kinds, each with a confidence computed by code:
    - `observed_by`: observation channels a counted fault mode lists (`documented_limit` when the range is a
      spec or setting range); confidence = the lower of the channel's and the fault mode's;
    - `named_by`: observation channels whose own name or description names the component as the thing
      measured; confidence = the channel's × `named_channel_factor` (0.5); flagged when it is the only O;
    - `threshold_events`: event channels a counted fault mode lists (the machine reports when a limit is
      reached); flagged.
  - **D** `driven_by`: operating inputs that drive a counted fault mode (recorded for M9; decides nothing).
  - **L** `life`, four bases: `stated` (a machine-document sentence or schedule row naming the component),
    `life_model` (a reference-document page its own fault modes cite, with a life-rating phrase such as L10),
    `declared` (machine.toml [[life]]) and `machine_wide` (a machine-wide statement on a page its fault modes
    cite; flagged "machine-wide, lower bound on life"). Only a statement that names the machine, or prints no
    word of any part's name, links through a cited page; a row naming another part (air p29 "Have all
    flexibles ... inspected") is linked to that part and gives no one a life (approved 2026-11-09).
  - `machine_life`: machine-wide statements, kept once per scope with the components they link to, for M9.
- **Steps** (`kaful/phases/p3_scope/`): `a_collect_signals`, `b_find_life_statements`, `c_decide`,
  `d_check`. `helpers/text.py` holds the naming rules: consecutive name forms, a designation in parentheses
  plus a name word, and `measures()` (a name followed by a quantity word, punctuation or nothing names the
  component as measured: "Coffee boiler temperature" does, "the coffee boiler flowmeter" does not).
- **Rule:** full_model = G and (O or L). connection_only for ancestors and flow-path neighbours of full
  models, and for G alone (flagged "documented fault mode, nothing to calibrate or observe"). Excluded
  otherwise. A full model whose only life is a life model and that has no O is flagged with M9's inputs:
  "L10 life model available, load ratings needed (declare in machine.toml [[life]] or look up)".
- **Gates** (`d_check`): one decision per component; a rule's full_model has G and (O or L); every
  [[scope]] and [[life]] entry names a known component. A failure fails the phase.
- **machine.toml** `[[scope]]` (override: component, decision, reason) and `[[life]]` (component, kind,
  value, unit). Each [[scope]] entry is recorded in the run manifest as an override of kind `scope`.
- **Review** (`p3_scope/tools/review.py`): `kaful understand review <machine> --run <scope run>` adds a
  "Scope decisions" section (flagged first, each signal with its evidence) and `scope` entries to the review
  record. A `scope` correction becomes a [[scope]] override, and a `life` addition a [[life]] declaration, on
  `review-apply`.
- **Scorer** (`p3_scope/tools/score.py`, `kaful scope score <machine> [--run]`): scored against the existing
  understand golden labels, with no new labelling. It reports recall given what understand extracted, the
  restated criterion 1, and strict and lenient precision. The score is saved to
  `outputs/scope_scores/<run_id>.json`.
- **Downstream:** `Scope.full_model_components(model)` is the only list per-component phases (M9, M10)
  iterate over; it fails if the scope names a component the model lacks.

## Decisions (Natalie, 2026-11-09)
- Schema 2 with signals, [[scope]] overrides, the G and (O or L) rule, and the 0.75 precision floor.
- L widened to life models in catalogues or standards, and to declared lives.
- Machine-wide statements are recorded, and count as L through a cited page (flagged as lower bounds).
- A second O path: channels that name the component, kept apart, at lower confidence, with tight matching.
- Threshold events count as O, as their own kind.
- Precision counts a full model as correct when its component has any golden `yes` fault mode, including
  `documented = false` rows (lenient); strict is reported too.
- **Exit criterion 1 restated:** every extracted component with a documented gradual fault mode is either
  full_model, or connection_only with the flag "nothing to calibrate or observe" (overridable by declaring a
  channel or a life). The original wording, full-model recall 1.0, assumed every such component is
  modelable; air_compressor drive_motor (overheating, no channel, no stated life) shows it isn't.
- Four air_compressor golden aliases were added, dated: compressor_oil ← atlas_copco_roto_injectfluid,
  restrictors ← restrictors_rf1_rf2, flexibles ← discharge_flexible, drive_motor_bearings ←
  bearing_grease_electric_motor.
- tiny_pump: the impeller under P1 is the right second full model, because the wear lives in the impeller.

  rerun shows `override`, the manifest records it) and **downstream** (`full_model_components`) are all
  tested offline. `pytest -q`: 654 passed. mypy is clean on `kaful/`.

## Cost
$0. Scope makes no LLM call, and no paid call was made in M8. The M1 target (eversys $0.16, CNC $0.07 of
per-component spend on components triage drops → $0) is now structural: no per-component phase exists yet,
and M9 and M10 must iterate `Scope.full_model_components`.

## Surprises and open issues
- **understand linked almost no observation channels to fault modes.** It linked none at all on eversys or
  CNC. So O rests mostly on the named-channel path and on threshold events, both flagged. Logged:
  understand.fault_modes should fill `observable_by` itself.
- **restrictors' L was attributed wrongly (fixed, approved 2026-11-09).** Before the fix, restrictors_rf1_rf2
  was full_model through the p29 (label 27) row "Have all flexibles/flexible joints inspected", which is about
  the flexibles: the row counted as machine-wide only because it doesn't print discharge_flexible's full name.
  Now that row links to discharge_flexible only and gives no life; restrictors is connection_only (flagged),
  and air recall is 5/9 (was 6/9 on bad evidence), precision 0.83 (was 0.86).
- **brewing_unit:** the checkpoint-3 preview was wrong. p84 "The service of Eversys coffee machines depends
  on the number of brewing chambers ... 12 months or every 50'000 products" is linked to brewing_unit by
  name ("brewing", from "brewing unit" without its generic head), not through a cited page, since its fault
  mode cites p52. Under the approved rule it does not count, so brewing_unit stays connection_only (flagged).
- **The threshold event on eversys is the generic `error_message` channel**, not W-014 alone, so the review
  shows which message the fault mode quotes.
- **The bearings' L is generic:** NSK pages naming the bearing type with an L10 or fatigue-life phrase, from
  a mounting guide. The flag asks for load ratings.
