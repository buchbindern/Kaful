# M10. Build (TwinDefinition and the ProgPy backend)

## The problem it addressed
M9 gave every full-model component a template family and priors, but nothing could run them. Legacy codegen
asked an LLM to write ProgPy classes. M10 builds each machine's twin deterministically from the Scope and the
ParamSet, and runs it through a ProgPy adapter that is the only place ProgPy is imported. No LLM is involved.

## What was built
- **TwinDefinition schema 2** (`kaful/artifacts/twin_definition.py`):
  - one binding per full-model component, with D in D_limit's unit and the component's other modes listed;
  - one failure event per binding;
  - every template condition is an input with its provenance: a channel, a nominal parameter, or undocumented
    (held at nominal, f = 1, flagged);
  - maintenance rules (replace, clean, descale), each with a trigger:
    - `schedule`: every interval;
    - `channel`: a category reported on an event channel;
    - `threshold`: replace when D reaches D_limit;
  - two stored profiles, `nominal` and `documented_maintenance`;
  - per-binding flags.
- **Build phase** (`kaful/phases/p5_build/`):
  - `a_bind_templates`;
  - `b_wire`: exact matching, sorted; no component-to-component quantities until M13;
  - `c_maintenance`: rules and profiles;
  - `d_check`, the gates: every full model bound with k and D_limit; every referenced parameter exists;
    D_limit's unit is D's; k's unit is "D unit / time unit"; channel dimensions match; connection units are
    compatible; recovery parameters exist; and the backend must build the twin.
- **Maintenance:**
  - Actions come from documented statements (replace, change → replace; clean, rinse → clean;
    descale → descale) and from machine.toml `[[maintenance]]`.
  - `[[maintenance]]` takes an interval in the time unit or in a calendar unit (converted through the duty
    cycle, flagged), a channel and category, or `on_threshold`.
  - replace sets D = 0; clean and descale set D × (1 − recovery). The recovery prior is uniform 0.9–1.0,
    source default, flagged; approved.
  - A threshold rule renews D at D_limit and counts each renewal in a state.
- **Cleaning intervals are lower bounds on life** (Natalie, 2026-11-10):
  - A documented or declared clean/descale interval is a `cleaning_interval` life statement. It sets the life
    prior through the rule `cleaning` (q 0.20, σ 0.35, like a consumable replacement) instead of
    life_judgement.
  - Gate: a component's final median time to D_limit, after `[[param]]`, may not be shorter than any of its
    cleaning intervals.
  - Calendar intervals now keep P(T < interval) = q under the combined spread, a correction to M9.
- **ProgPy adapter** (`kaful/backends/progpy/`):
  - One flat PrognosticsModel per machine, created from data, with sorted states, inputs, outputs and events.
    ProgPy's CompositeModel is not used, because of its hash-order issue (M2).
  - Rates come from the pure-numpy templates.
  - Channel values are converted to template units with pint once, at render time.
  - Resets arrive as inputs held for one step; threshold renewal happens inside `next_state`.
  - `simulate` runs ProgPy's `simulate_to` with fixed parameter values (prior medians by default) and no
    noise. `estimate` and `predict` raise "M13".
  - The backend is loaded by name (`kaful.backends.load_backend`), so importing the pipeline never loads ProgPy.
- **Shared code:** `kaful/lifetime.py` holds the duty cycle, calendar conversion, maintenance actions and
  declared intervals, used by both parameterize and build. Parameterize adds recovery priors.

## Decisions (Natalie, 2026-11-10)

  - no LLM template step;
  - only the templates that in-scope components need;
  - the CNC bearings run on bearing_fatigue with judged lives, flagged;
  - exact wiring and unit checks now, fuzzy matching later.

## Results
Final runs: eversys de19f9, air_compressor 27f35c, CNC 8c2f37. All pass, at $0.

**Calibration.** At prior medians each failure event fires at the median life: eversys within 0.01%,
air_compressor within 0.07%, CNC within 0.02% (a time step of 1/500 of the shortest life).

**eversys** (products):

| Component | Life prior: median (5–95%) | From | No maintenance | Documented maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| milk_system | 343 (81–1,440) | daily clean = 164 products, calendar, flagged | fails at 343 | never fails; peak D 0.50 of the limit |
| steam_wand | 10,430 (2,480–43,840) | monthly clean = 5,000 products, calendar, flagged | fails at 10,430 | never fails; peak D 0.51 |
| water_filter | 13,900 | judged capacity 1,669 L at 0.12 L per product | first W-014 at 13,900, then renewed | the same: renewed at the limit, 2 renewals over 40,000 products |
| coffee_boiler, steam_boiler, top_piston | judged | life_judgement v2 | fail at their medians | no maintenance documented (flagged) |

**air_compressor** (30,000 h of documented maintenance):
- The air filter (cleaned every 500 h, replaced every 4,000 h), the oil (4,000 h) and the oil separator
  (8,000 h) never fail.
- air_cooler and oil_cooler fail at their medians: nothing documents their maintenance.

**CNC:** the bearings and spindle_motor run on judged lives. They have no maintenance, and their inputs are
undocumented, so f = 1.

**Tests** (offline):
- binding, units and gates;
- channel wiring (double flow halves the life);
- scheduled replacement; a clean at recovery 0.5 that halves D; threshold renewal; calendar and threshold
  declarations;
- cleaning-interval priors and their gate;
- unit conversions (°C → K, L/min → m³/h);
- `BuildError` naming the component;
- determinism under several hash seeds, for both the TwinDefinition and the simulation;
- ProgPy isolation.

`pytest -q`: 698 passed. mypy is clean on `kaful/`.

## Cost
$0. M10 has no LLM step: template selection has been code since M9, which meets PLAN's M1 target (≥80% cut)
at 100%. The parameterize reruns were served from the response cache.

## Surprises
- **No component-to-component connections exist yet.** The templates output only damage and health, and
  understand linked almost no driving channels, so almost every condition is undocumented (f = 1).
  Observation models in M13 bring the first quantities to wire.
- **milk_system's judged life (93 products) was shorter than its daily clean (164 products at the default
  duty).** The cleaning-interval rule replaced the judgement with a prior consistent with the documented
  cleaning (median 343).
- **A calendar-prior inconsistency from M9** (P(T < interval) above q under the widened spread) surfaced
  through the existing P(T < interval) gate. It is fixed and recorded in M9.md.
- **Two test failures, both fixed:**
  - The M4 import-walk test failed when the build phase imported the adapter. The fix loads the backend by
    name, so importing the pipeline never loads ProgPy; the test now skips only the adapter package, as its
    docstring says.
  - One commit (14ad8a1) went in with that test failing; the fix is dd45355.
- **A point-value recovery** would have needed a wider exception in a pinned point-value rule. It became a real
  distribution instead (uniform 0.9–1.0), approved.
