1 Monitoring
TP-300 Booster Pump Skid (fictional test machine). The controller logs the following
channels once every 10 seconds:
  suction_pressure (bar), measuring range -1 to 5
  motor_current (A), measuring range 0 to 16 for the TP-300-15 and 0 to 25 for the TP-300-22
  bearing_temperature (degC)
Example: with a clean strainer the suction_pressure reads about 1.2 bar at rated flow.
