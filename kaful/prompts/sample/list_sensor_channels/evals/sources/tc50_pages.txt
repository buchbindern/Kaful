TC-50 Process Chiller - Quick Reference (fictional test machine)
4 Monitoring and alarms
The chiller controller logs these values every 30 s:
  supply_temp (degC), chilled water leaving the evaporator, range -10 to 30
  return_temp (degC), chilled water returning from the process, range -10 to 40
  condenser_pressure (bar), range 0 to 30
  compressor_current (A), range 0 to 60
Alarm limits
  High condenser pressure: trip above 24 bar.
  Low supply temperature: trip below 2 degC (freeze protection).
  Compressor overcurrent: trip above 45 A.
Rated cooling capacity is 50 kW at 7 degC supply temperature.

5 Maintenance
Clean the condenser coil every 1000 operating hours. Check the refrigerant charge once a year.
The compressor is rated for 40000 operating hours.
